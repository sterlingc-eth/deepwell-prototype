Security & Privacy

Last reviewed: September 28, 2026

This page is the plain-language answer to the question every new customer asks: can you see our data or files? It describes the real, current controls behind DeepWell — how your documents are isolated, encrypted, and accessed — so you don't have to take our word for it without knowing why. For the legal version, see the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

Your data is isolated per account

Every document, customer record, and answer lives under your company's own account. The database enforces this at the row level for every single query — not just in application code that could have a bug in it — so one customer's records are never returned by a request made under another customer's account, even by mistake.

Encryption

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) everywhere, including between DeepWell and the providers listed below. Document files and database records are encrypted at rest by those providers (Cloudflare and Neon) as a standard part of their storage.

File access is private and short-lived

Uploaded files are not public. Every time a document is viewed or downloaded, DeepWell issues a signed link scoped to that one file, for a short window of time, rather than a permanent public URL — so a leaked link can't be replayed indefinitely and a file can't be found by guessing a URL.

Support access is yours to grant

Our support team does not have standing access to your documents. When you want our help — troubleshooting an issue, for example — you grant temporary support access from your account settings, and you can revoke it at any time. Every grant, and everything accessed under it, is recorded in your account's activity log so there's a record of who looked at what, and when. In an emergency, such as an outage, staff can access an account without a grant; that access requires a written reason and always appears in the same log. Separately, the two founders administer the hosting, database and file-storage accounts that DeepWell runs on; that infrastructure access is outside the in-product log.

Audit logs

Account-level actions — uploads, edits, exports, and the support access above — are written to an activity log scoped to your account, so you can see what happened on your account and when.

Your data, your control

A company admin can export the company's data or delete it entirely, at any time, from Team → Settings — you don't need to email us and wait. Deleting the company also cancels its subscription. Deleting a document removes it from both the file vault and the search index within our normal processing cycle.

AI processing and training

To read documents and answer questions, DeepWell sends document content and query text to our AI providers (Anthropic, and Voyage AI for search) for processing. Under those providers' API terms, this content is not used to train their models, and it is not shared with any other customer. Answers include a citation back to the source document — but AI-generated answers can be wrong, so always check the citation; see the Terms & Conditions for the full disclaimer.

Reporting a security concern

If you believe you've found a security issue, please tell us before telling anyone else: security@deepwelltechnology.com. We'll acknowledge real reports and work with you on a fix.
