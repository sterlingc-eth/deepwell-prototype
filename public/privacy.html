Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

This policy explains what DeepWell Inc. ("DeepWell Technology," "we," "us") collects when you use our document management and AI question-answering service, why we collect it, and what control you have over it. It applies to deepwelltechnology.com and the DeepWell application.

What we collect

  • Account information — name, work email, company name, and role, collected when you or your team sign up and authenticate.
  • Usage data — pages viewed, questions asked (not the document content itself, tracked separately below), timestamps, and device/browser type, used to keep the service running and to fix problems.
  • Billing information — handled directly by our payment processor (Stripe, once payments launch); we do not store your card number.

Documents you upload

The work orders, invoices, warranty sheets, photos, and other records you upload are stored under your company's own account and are never pooled with another customer's data. We process the content of these documents so the product can extract facts (serials, dates, addresses, amounts) and answer your team's questions about them. You retain ownership of everything you upload — see the Terms & Conditions for details.

AI processing

To read and extract information from your documents, and to generate answers to questions, DeepWell sends document content and query text to Anthropic's AI models for processing. This is done to provide the service you asked for — it is not used to train Anthropic's or anyone else's models, and your documents are not shared with any other customer. Answers are generated from your records and include a citation back to the source document, but AI-generated answers can be wrong; see the Terms & Conditions for the AI output disclaimer.

Retention and deletion

You can delete any document from your account at any time; deletion removes it from both the vault (Cloudflare R2) and the index (Neon Postgres) within our normal processing cycle. You can request full account deletion at any time by contacting us below — we will remove your company's account data and stored documents, except where we're required to retain limited records for legal or accounting reasons.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. Document storage is scoped per account so one customer's files are never visible to another. Access to production data is limited to the people who need it to operate the service.

Cookies

DeepWell does not use tracking or advertising cookies, and does not show a cookie banner. The only cookies we set are strictly-necessary authentication cookies from Clerk, used to keep you signed in. Site analytics (Vercel Web Analytics) are cookieless and do not identify individual visitors, so no consent banner is required under GDPR or CCPA for either of these.

Children

DeepWell is a business tool for HVAC contractors and is not directed to, or intended for use by, anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product changes. Material changes will be reflected in the effective date above, and significant changes will be communicated to account owners directly.
