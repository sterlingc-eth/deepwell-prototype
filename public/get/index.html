Install in under a minute

Pick your phone. Then sign in with the same account you use on the computer.

  3. 3Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. 4Open DeepWell from your home screen and sign in. The home-screen app keeps its own sign-in, so do this once inside the app.

  1. 1For the office, use the full app at deepwelltechnology.com/app. It has everything: Inbox, Records, Dashboard, Team and Billing.

Questions

Does it cost extra?

No. The phone app is included with every DeepWell plan. Your techs sign in with their own DeepWell accounts.

Why isn't it in the App Store or Google Play?

It installs from the browser, so everyone always has the latest version with nothing to download or update. It gets its own icon and opens full screen like any other app.

How do my techs get access?

The shop owner invites them from Team in the desktop app. Once they accept the invite, they open this page on their phone and follow the steps above.

Does it work with bad signal?

It's built for weak signal: the app opens quickly, photos are shrunk on the phone before uploading, and slow answers show a retry instead of spinning forever. Asking questions and uploading still need a connection.
