Jobs, permits and invoices in one place.

Contracts, permits, purchase orders, invoices and inspection reports are filed by customer and address. People in the field can scan paperwork from a phone, and anyone on the team can ask Donovan, DeepWell’s assistant, and get the answer with the original page attached.

What DeepWell does today

Works today

Reads the paperwork behind every job

Contracts, permits, purchase orders, invoices and inspection reports, in whatever format they’re in. Handwriting and rough scans are fine.

Works today

Files each page under the customer and the address

DeepWell pulls out the names, addresses, dates, amounts and document numbers, so the contract, the permit and the invoices for one address are found together. The original page is kept.

Works today

Scan from the job site

Scan paperwork on your phone, even with no signal. The scan is saved on the phone and uploads by itself when the signal returns.
