# Customer outreach — automated warranty-upsell email

Built per the owner's ask: "automate emails to customers close to being up
on their warranties and who qualify for extended warranty."

## What it is

A "Customer outreach" card on the Dashboard + a new Outreach screen (reached
from that card or `?screen=outreach&equipment=<id>`, not in the primary nav).
Reuses the same warranty-tier math the bell/dashboard already show
(`api/warranty-attention.js`) to find units at `expiring-90`, `expiring-30`,
or `expired`, and drafts one plain-English email per (unit, tier) offering an
extended warranty / maintenance agreement. **Off by default** — nothing is
ever emailed until an admin turns it on (Outreach screen → Settings).

Two modes: **Review first** (default) — drafts sit for an admin to Approve/
Skip, then click "Send approved". **Automatic** — the nightly cron approves
and sends new drafts itself, up to 50 per tenant per run.

## Compliance (CAN-SPAM)

- Every email carries a mandatory footer: "You're receiving this because
  `<shop>` serviced your equipment. Reply STOP to opt out." — not optional,
  baked into `renderOutreachEmail()` (`api/_lib/outreach.js`), covered by
  `scripts/verify-outreach.mjs`.
- **Gap**: CAN-SPAM also requires a physical postal address in the footer.
  `tenant_outreach_settings` has no address field yet — the footer includes
  one only if a caller supplies it, which nothing does today. **Before
  relying on this for real customers**, add an address field and pass it
  into `renderOutreachEmail`'s `address` param.
- "Reply STOP" isn't wired to an inbound mailbox (no email-receiving webhook
  exists here). The `optOut` op (`{op:'optOut', customerId}`) is honored at
  send time, but something has to call it — a person today, an inbound-email
  integration later.
