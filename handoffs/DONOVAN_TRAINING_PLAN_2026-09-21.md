# Donovan pre-launch training plan (2026-09-21)

Goal: make Donovan consistent on business questions before launch, at near-zero cost, and only claim accuracy we've measured.

## What "training" means here
We do not retrain Haiku. We train the system around it:
1. **Question bank** — thousands of natural-language questions with known answers, generated from the synthetic business answer key.
2. **Normalization layer** — cheap code that cleans sloppy input before the classifier (typos, abbreviations, no punctuation).
3. **Few-shot examples** — ~25 tricky question→plan pairs in the planner prompt.
4. **Miss loop** — log every fallthrough / "can't filter by X yet", review weekly, add to the bank, fix in code.
5. **Targeted gap corpus** — a few hundred documents only where coverage is missing.

## Question bank design (the important part)
Generated by `scripts/gen-question-bank.mjs` from `test-docs/business-small/ANSWER_KEY.json` — every question carries its expected route (analytics / lookup / retrieval), expected plan (entity, filters, timeRange) and expected answer value.

**Categories (what office staff, dispatchers and owners actually ask):**
- Counts: customers / units / jobs / invoices by city, county, state, zip, brand, month, year, technician
- Lists: "which customers in Chandler", "list our Trane units", "who has a unit older than 15 years"
- Lookups: phone / email / address / serial / model / install date for one customer or address
- History: "last time we were at 123 Main", "what did we do for Ramirez in July"
- Warranty: "whose warranty expires this year", "is the Nguyen unit still under warranty"
- Maintenance due: "who hasn't had service in 12 months"
- Data hygiene: "customers missing email / phone", "documents needing review"
- Technician: "what did Mike do last week", "jobs per tech this month"
- Comparisons and trends: "Trane vs Carrier", "jobs per month this year"
- Money (flag — needs invoice totals support): "how much did we invoice in Q2", "biggest customer by revenue"

**Sloppiness variants per question (natural language, not exact):**
- lowercase, no punctuation, no question mark
- abbreviations: az, nv, cust, custs, ppl, hvac, ac, mo, yr, tech, #, qty
- typos within 1 edit of a known word (custmers, arizna, carrer, warrenty)
- statement forms: "show me…", "pull up…", "list…", "give me…", "need the…"
- voice-to-text style: "hey how many customers we got in maricopa"
- trailing context: "…for the newsletter", "…so I can call them"
- filler: "can you tell me", "quick question", "real quick"

**Normalization layer (`api/_lib/nlNormalize.js`, $0):** lowercase, strip filler and punctuation, expand abbreviation table, fuzzy-correct tokens to the known vocabulary (entity synonyms, brands, cities, counties, states, months, field names) at edit distance ≤1 for words ≥5 letters. Runs before the classifier and the planner; the original text is still shown to the user.

## Acceptance before launch
- Bank ≥ 3,000 questions; classifier routes ≥ 99% correctly (deterministic, measured in verify).
- Haiku planner sample (500): ≥ 97% correct plan; every miss either fixed in code or answered with the honest fallback — never a wrong number.
- Live sample (300): answer value matches answer key ≥ 97%; 0 confidently-wrong numbers.
- Website copy: no accuracy percentage for business questions until these numbers exist; document lookup keeps its measured claim.

## Timeline
- Day 1: generator + normalization layer + bank wired into verify-analytics; first pass numbers.
- Day 2: few-shot set, fix misses, live sample, miss-loop logging, doc the results.
- Weekly after launch: 30-min miss review.

## Not doing (and why)
- Fine-tuning: not available for Haiku on our plan and wouldn't fix dropped-condition bugs; cost and iteration speed are worse than code fixes.
- Thousands of generic documents: teach nothing new, cost credits; documents aren't the bottleneck, questions are.
