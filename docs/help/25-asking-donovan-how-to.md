Donovan answers questions about your own records on the **Ask** screen (desktop) or **Ask** tab (phone).

### How do I ask a question?
~ how do i ask, how do i ask a question, where do i ask donovan, type a question, ask box, ask screen, ask donovan anything, what do i type, how do i use ask, enter to ask, new line in ask box, clear the ask box, typeahead, suggestions as i type
!covers:A-ASK
Open **Ask**, type in the box ("Ask Donovan anything — an address, a serial, a name, a question…") and press **Enter**. **Shift+Enter** starts a new line and **Esc** clears the box. Suggestions appear as you type, and the small pill under the box says what Donovan will search. Plain wording works; you don't need a special format.

### Where do I get example questions or my recent ones?
~ example questions, try asking, sample questions, what can i ask, ideas for questions, recent questions, past questions, ask again, re-ask a question, history of questions, what should i ask, starter questions
!covers:A-ASK-EXAMPLES
Before your first question the Ask screen shows **Try asking** chips built from your own records (or "For example" samples) and a **Recent** list of past questions you can click to ask again. Field view uses tech-style examples.

### How do I see where an answer came from?
~ sources, citations, where did that come from, show the source, source document, open the source, proof, check the source, how do i verify an answer, cited documents, closest documents, numbered markers, records behind a count, show me the rows, show me the documents behind that
!covers:A-ASK-SOURCES
Every answer carries citations: numbered [1] markers and source chips. Click one to open the document. When nothing is found it shows "Closest documents", and for counts a records panel lists the rows behind the number.

### What does Include unverified do?
~ include unverified, unverified, searched verified records only, searched linked and verified records, verified only, why didnt it find my new document, document not in answer, new upload not showing in answers, search unchecked documents, include documents not checked, donovan didnt find my new upload, didnt find my new upload, new upload not found, donovan cant find my new document
!covers:A-ASK-UNVERIFIED
By default Donovan searches checked (verified) records. Turn on **Include unverified** to also search documents that were read but not checked yet. The caption under the answer says "Searched verified records only" or "Searched linked and verified records". Treat unverified facts with care.

### Can I ask a follow-up question?
~ follow up question, followup, ask a follow up, follow up, continue the conversation, ask more about this, new question, start over, start a new question, clear the conversation, following up on earlier questions, related questions, does donovan remember
!covers:A-ASK-FOLLOWUP
Yes. Type the next question and Donovan builds on the last four ("Following up on N earlier questions"). Click a follow-up chip under an answer for suggestions. **New question** starts fresh.

### Donovan says "Nothing in your records answers that." What now?
~ nothing in your records answers that, not on file, no answer found, donovan found nothing, did you mean, closest documents, why no answer, cant find, doesnt know, didnt find anything, no results, donovan cant find my customer, no record found, not found
!covers:A-ASK-NOANSWER
That means the records don't contain it, or it isn't checked yet. Look at the **Did you mean** chips and "Closest documents", try **Include unverified**, or check that the paperwork was uploaded and finished (Inbox). A **Not on file** badge marks the miss.

### How do I tell Donovan an answer was wrong?
~ was this right, thumbs up, thumbs down, report wrong answer, give feedback, wrong answer button, what was wrong, rate an answer, flag an answer, mark answer wrong, donovan got it wrong, tell donovan its wrong
!covers:A-ASK-FEEDBACK
Under an answer use **Was this right?**: thumbs up or thumbs down. A thumbs-down opens "What was wrong? (optional)"; it is logged and Donovan re-checks. Then open the cited source and correct the record if a field was misread.

### How do I share or copy an answer?
~ share an answer, copy an answer, send an answer, share answer with my tech, copy the answer, copy a serial, copy button, share button on answer, text an answer, email an answer, export an answer
!covers:A-ASK-SHARE
Use **Share** on an answer to copy or share it, and the copy button next to a value to copy just that value.

### How do I ask about a serial number from a photo?
~ serial from photo, photo of the label, scan a serial, camera icon, take a photo of the label, read a nameplate, look up a unit from a photo, scan nameplate to ask, serial number camera, identify a unit from its nameplate, sn xxx 000000
!covers:A-ASK-SERIAL
On the desktop Ask screen tap the camera icon in the box ("Serial from photo"), **Take a photo of the label**, then confirm or edit the serial it read. Donovan then asks about that serial. On the phone you can also photograph a nameplate on the **Scan** tab to file it.

### Donovan shows an error. What does it mean?
~ donovan couldnt get an answer, ask error, error asking, that took too long, daily ai budget reached, unusually high usage, session has expired, reload the page, see plans on ask, ask not working, ask failed, donovan not responding, donovan error message, error in ask, couldnt get an answer, ask says donovan couldnt get an answer, donovan didnt work
!covers:A-ASK-ERR
"Donovan couldn't get an answer" plus a reason: try again. "Your session has expired. Reload the page and sign in again." "Daily AI budget reached — resumes tomorrow." "Donovan is seeing unusually high usage on your account" means email support@deepwelltechnology.com. A billing message shows **See plans**. On the phone, "That took too long — probably a weak signal. Try again."
