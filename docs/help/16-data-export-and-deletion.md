You can also ask us at privacy@deepwelltechnology.com, and we will remove your company's account data and stored documents, except where we're required to keep limited records for legal or accounting reasons. ### What is kept after deletion?
~ what is kept, what is retained, retention, what remains, do you keep my data, backups after delete, stripe records, billing records, after deletion, data retention, how long do you keep data, kept after delete, what do you keep, what do you keep after i delete, keep after i delete, still keep after deleting, what stays after deleting
Deletion empties the company's documents, extracted facts and activity data. We keep a minimal account record (the company name or ID, plan and billing status), a deletion receipt and Stripe's own billing records, which we can't erase from Stripe. Database backups age out on the provider's retention window. Deletion doesn't remove people's sign-in accounts, which are removed separately.

### What happens to my data if I cancel?

You may request an export or deletion after canceling. We may suspend an account for non-payment or a terms violation, and after termination you can request an export for a reasonable period before it is deleted. Export from Team → Settings while you still have access.
