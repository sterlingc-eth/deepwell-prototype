You can delete a document, or the whole shop's data, without waiting on us.

### How do I delete a document?
~ delete a document, delete a file, remove a document, remove a file, delete record, delete an upload, get rid of a document, erase a document, remove upload
Delete it from the document in the app. Deletion removes it from both the file vault and the search index within our normal processing cycle.

### How do I delete all my data or close my account?
~ delete my data, delete all my data, delete my account, close my account, remove my account, erase my data, wipe my data, close account, terminate account, delete everything, data deletion, account deletion, delete the shop, gdpr delete, right to be forgotten, permanently delete
A shop admin can delete the shop's data from **Team → Settings**. Deleting the shop also **cancels its subscription first**, and if that fails, nothing is deleted. Export your data first if you want a copy. You can also ask us at privacy@deepwelltechnology.com. If you want a person to handle it, I can send your request to the team.

### What is kept after deletion?
~ what is kept, what is retained, retention, what remains, do you keep my data, backups after delete, stripe records, billing records, after deletion, data retention, how long do you keep data, kept after delete, what do you keep, what do you keep after i delete, keep after i delete, still keep after deleting, what stays after deleting
Deletion empties the shop's documents, extracted facts and activity data. We keep a minimal account record (the shop name or ID, plan and billing status), a deletion receipt and Stripe's own billing records, which we can't erase from Stripe. Database backups age out on the provider's retention window. Deletion doesn't remove people's sign-in accounts, which are removed separately.

### What happens to my data if I cancel?
~ data after cancel, my data if i cancel, cancel and data, what if i leave, leaving deepwell, if i stop paying, data after cancellation, after cancellation, canceled account data
You may request an export or deletion after canceling. We may suspend an account for non-payment or a terms violation, and after termination you can request an export for a reasonable period before it is deleted. Export from Team → Settings while you still have access.
