Plan changes, cards and invoices are handled in Billing, through Stripe's billing portal. Only shop admins can open it.

### How do I upgrade or change my plan?
~ upgrade, downgrade, change plan, switch plan, move to shop, move to crew, upgrade my plan, change my subscription, switch tiers, bigger plan, smaller plan, go from solo to shop
Open **Billing** in the app and choose the plan you want, or use **Manage billing**. Only a shop admin (or the owner of a solo account) can change the plan. If you're not sure which plan fits, ask me or email support@deepwelltechnology.com.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
~ cancel, cancel my subscription, cancel my account, cancel my plan, how do i cancel, stop my subscription, end my subscription, unsubscribe, cancel membership, stop billing, terminate
Admins can cancel from **Billing → Manage billing**. A monthly plan can be canceled any time and takes effect at the end of the current billing period. There are no prorated refunds for partial months. If you want a person to handle it, I can send your request to the team.
!handoff:cancel

### What is your refund policy?
~ refund, refunds, money back, get a refund, refund policy, prorated refund, charged by mistake, billed in error, wrong charge, dispute a charge, chargeback, double charged, charged twice, overcharged
Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months. Annual plans are refundable in full if you cancel within 30 days of the first annual payment, and non-refundable after that. Records Rescue is non-refundable once scanning has begun. If you think you were billed in error, contact billing@deepwelltechnology.com within 30 days of the charge. I can't issue refunds myself.
!handoff:refund

### Where do I find my invoices or receipts?
~ invoice, invoices, receipt, receipts, past invoices, download invoice, billing history, payment history, billing statement, tax invoice, vat, w9, need an invoice
Open **Billing → Manage billing** as an admin. Stripe's portal lists your invoices and receipts. If you can't find one, email billing@deepwelltechnology.com.

### How do I update my credit card?
~ update card, change credit card, new card, change payment method, update payment info, card expired, update billing info, change billing email, billing address, add a card
As an admin, open **Billing → Manage billing** and update your payment method there. Card details are handled by Stripe. DeepWell never sees or stores your card number. Please don't type a card number into this chat.

### Do you offer refunds for the free trial?
~ trial refund, charged after trial, charged after free trial, was charged after trial, forgot to cancel trial
You aren't charged during the 30-day Solo trial, and you can cancel before it ends. If you were charged and believe it was a mistake, email billing@deepwelltechnology.com within 30 days of the charge.
!handoff:refund
