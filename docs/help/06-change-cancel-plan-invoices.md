Plan changes, cards and invoices are handled in Billing, through Stripe's billing portal. Only shop admins can open it.

### How do I upgrade or change my plan?
~ upgrade, downgrade, change plan, switch plan, move to shop, move to crew, upgrade my plan, change my subscription, switch tiers, bigger plan, smaller plan, go from solo to shop, choose a plan, monthly or annual, how do i pick a plan, pick a plan, where do i change my plan, plan change, current plan
!covers:A-PLAN-CHANGE
An admin opens **Billing → Choose a plan**, picks **Monthly** or **Annual · 1 month free**, then presses **Choose plan** on the tier they want. Your current tier shows **Current plan**. Payment happens on Stripe's secure checkout page. Members can open Billing but can't change the plan. Not sure which plan fits? Ask me or email support@deepwelltechnology.com.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
~ cancel, cancel my subscription, cancel my account, cancel my plan, how do i cancel, stop my subscription, end my subscription, unsubscribe, cancel membership, stop billing, terminate, where do i cancel, cancel in the app, cancel button, how to cancel, cancle, how do i cancle, cancle my plan
!covers:A-CANCEL
An admin opens **Billing → Manage billing** (in the Current plan card), which opens Stripe's billing portal, and cancels there. A monthly plan can be canceled any time and takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and Billing then shows "Ends <date>". There are no prorated refunds for partial months. Want a person to handle it? I can send your request to the team.
!handoff:cancel

### Can I cancel my plan at any time?
~ cancel, cancel my subscription, cancel my account, cancel my plan, how do i cancel, stop my subscription, end my subscription, unsubscribe, cancel membership, stop billing, terminate, cancel anytime, can i cancel, cancellation policy, cancel before renewal, cancel any time
You can cancel at any time. A monthly plan's cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and there are no prorated refunds for partial months. The 30-day Solo trial can also be canceled any time, and you aren't charged until it ends. If you'd like a person to handle it, I can send your request to the team.
!handoff:cancel
!audience:public-only
!source:terms.html,index.html

### What is your refund policy?
~ refund, refunds, money back, get a refund, refund policy, prorated refund, charged by mistake, billed in error, wrong charge, dispute a charge, chargeback, double charged, charged twice, overcharged
Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months. Annual plans are refundable in full if you cancel within 30 days of the first annual payment, and non-refundable after that. Records Rescue is non-refundable once scanning has begun. If you believe you were billed in error, contact us within 30 days of the charge at support@deepwelltechnology.com. I can't issue refunds myself.
!handoff:refund
!audience:public
!source:terms.html

### Where do I find my invoices or receipts?
~ invoice, invoices, receipt, receipts, past invoices, download invoice, billing history, payment history, billing statement, tax invoice, vat, w9, need an invoice, past bills, see my past bills, my bills, see my bills
!covers:A-INVOICES
An admin opens **Billing → Manage billing**. Stripe's portal lists every invoice and receipt to view or download. Members can't open the portal. If one is missing, email billing@deepwelltechnology.com.

### How do I update my credit card?
~ update card, change credit card, new card, change payment method, update payment info, card expired, update billing info, change billing email, billing address, add a card
!covers:A-CARD
An admin opens **Billing → Manage billing** and updates the payment method in Stripe's portal. Card details are handled by Stripe; DeepWell never sees or stores your card number. Please don't type a card number into this chat.

### Does DeepWell store my credit card?
~ store my card, store credit card, card number, is my card safe, credit card security, do you keep my card, card details, stripe, payment security, is payment secure, who processes payments, payment processor
No. Stripe, our payment processor, handles and stores your card details and processes payments directly. DeepWell stores your subscription and billing status and a Stripe customer ID, never your card number. Please don't type a card number into this chat.
!audience:public
!source:privacy.html

### Do you offer refunds for the free trial?
~ trial refund, charged after trial, charged after free trial, was charged after trial, forgot to cancel trial
You aren't charged during the 30-day Solo trial (a card is required to start), and you can cancel any time before it ends. If you were charged and believe it was a mistake, contact us within 30 days of the charge at support@deepwelltechnology.com.
