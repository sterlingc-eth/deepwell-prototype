When DeepWell can't be sure of something it leaves a short job in **Inbox → Needs you**. Anyone on the team can fix and verify records. Deleting a document and merging customers are admin only.

### Where do I fix a document that needs info?
~ fix a document, needs info, needs your attention, needs a person, where are the flagged documents, flagged documents, where do i fix, where do i review, review queue, documents that need review, needs you, inbox needs you, where is the needs info queue, pending questions, missing information queue, clear the inbox, what do the chips mean, filter chips in inbox
!covers:A-NEEDS-YOU
Open **Inbox** and choose the **Needs you** tab (the badge is the count). Chips narrow the list: **Decisions** (quick questions), **Needs a person**, **Missing info**, **Needs linking**, **Conflicts**, **Duplicates**, **Ready to verify**, **Shop records**, **Money to check** (only when there are money issues) and **All**. Pick one, open a document and work through it. The Dashboard's Data health tiles open the matching chip.

### How do I answer DeepWell's questions in Decisions?
~ decisions, answer questions, answer a question, decision card, exception queue, pick an option, which customer, which address, confirm the serial, type it instead, snooze, dismiss, doesnt apply, ask again later, why are we asking, questions from deepwell, respond to deepwell, answer deepwell
!covers:A-DECISIONS
**Inbox → Needs you → Decisions** shows one card per question. Click the right option (or press 1 to 9). If none fits choose **Type it instead**, type the value and press **Confirm**. **Why are we asking?** shows the evidence. The clock button asks again later (S), and the x means it doesn't apply. The "On file for this document" chips have a check (**Looks right**) and a pencil (**Fix**). When it's empty: "Nothing needs a decision right now."

### How do I correct a wrong serial number or other field?
~ correct a field, fix a serial, wrong serial, serial number wrong, fix the serial, fix a mistake, misread, wrong value, wrong name, wrong address, wrong date, edit a field, change a field, edit extracted data, edit a document, fix wrong information, correct the model number, fix a typo, edit the serial number, misread serial, how do i fix it, data is wrong, wrong info, address was read wrong, the address was read wrong how do i change it, read wrong how do i change it, how do i change it
!covers:A-FIX-FIELD
Open the document from **Inbox → Needs you** (or **Records → Documents**). Under **Extracted fields**, fix the value; a required field that's missing has a box and an **Add** button. On a Decisions card use the pencil (**Fix**) beside the field. Your correction is used for later answers. On the phone open the document from **Docs** and answer under **Needs your input**.

### How do I mark a document as checked or verified?
~ verify a document, mark checked, mark as verified, approve, approve a document, advance, verify, confirm, looks right, confirm a value, verified, checked, mark as checked, accept a document, confirm the data, sign off, how do i verify, finish a document, blocked, cant advance, why cant i approve
!covers:A-VERIFY-FACT
Click **Looks right** (the check) on a field you've confirmed. When nothing is left to resolve, the button at the bottom of the document reads **Mark checked** (or **Advance to <stage>**). If it's greyed out, a line says what's blocking: choose a document type, add the missing field, link it to a record or settle a disputed value. Checked documents count toward answers.

### How do I set or change a document's type?
~ document type, change type, wrong type, set the type, classify, classify a document, unclassified, what type of document, mark as invoice, mark as warranty, change document type, its an invoice not a work order, wrong document type, choose a type
!covers:A-DOC-TYPE
Open the document and use the **Document type** buttons in its review panel. The required fields update to match the type. A document with no type shows as "Unclassified".

### How do I link a document to a customer or a unit?
~ link a document, link to a customer, link to customer, assign to a customer, attach to a customer, needs linking, not linked, unlinked, change customer, wrong customer, link to equipment, link to a unit, connect a document, create a new customer, two customers named, ambiguous customer, which customer, move a document to another customer, attach a document to a different customer, different customer, attach a document to a customer, move to a different customer
!covers:A-LINK
Open the document. In **Customer** press **Link to <name>** if DeepWell suggests one, or **Change customer…**, search by name and press **Use this**, or type a name under "Or create a new customer…". Under **Linked to** you can also pick a record in "Record to link" and press **Link**. If two customers share a name it asks you to confirm which one. From a customer's profile you can also **Assign a document to this customer**.

### Two documents disagree. What do I do?
~ conflict, conflicts, two documents disagree, documents disagree, disputed value, different serial numbers, values dont match, mismatch, contradiction, which one is right, resolve a conflict, conflicting information, disagree on
!covers:A-CONFLICT
Open the **Conflicts** chip. The panel says "Two documents disagree on <field>" and shows each value with its source. Click the one that is right. Open each source first if you're not sure.

### What do I do with a duplicate document?
~ duplicate document, duplicates, same document twice, uploaded twice, merge into original, duplicate file, duplicate upload, already uploaded, double upload, same file, remove duplicate, why is it a duplicate, held out of counts
!covers:A-DUP-DOC
Open **Inbox → Needs you → Duplicates**, open the copy and press **Merge into original**. The original stays and the copy is dropped, so nothing is double counted. An upload with identical contents is caught automatically ("Already on file").

### How do I merge duplicate customers?
~ merge customers, duplicate customers, duplicate customer, same customer twice, merge two customers, combine customers, two records for one customer, customer listed twice, merge into, not the same, keep separate, merge all, undo a merge, dedupe customers, clean up customers, customer twice, merge button not working, merge button greyed out, tech cant merge, only admins can merge, why is the merge button not working, merge button not working for my tech
!covers:A-DUP-CUSTOMER
Admins can merge. In **Records → Customers**, a banner lists likely pairs: **Merge into <name>** (tick which one to keep), **Not the same**, or **Merge all**. Admins also get a whole-shop **Duplicate customers** scan there, with Undo after a merge. The same suggestions appear in Inbox → Needs you. A different name at the same address is never merged automatically.

### What does Verify with AI do?
~ verify with ai, ai verify, ai verified, let ai check, verified by ai, ai check, auto verify, what is ai verified, automatic verification, use ai to verify
!covers:A-AI-VERIFY
On a document that isn't checked yet, **Verify with AI** has DeepWell re-check it. You'll see "Verified by AI." or "Not confident enough yet — this still needs a person." AI-verified documents show an **AI verified** badge.

### How do I delete a document?
~ delete a document, delete document, remove a document, delete a file, delete a record, remove an upload, delete an upload, wrong file uploaded, delete this doc, get rid of a document, undo an upload, uploaded the wrong file, erase a document, delete one document, uploaded the wrong file how do i remove it, remove a file i uploaded, remove the wrong file
!covers:A-DELETE-DOC
An admin opens the document (**Inbox → Needs you**, then the document) and presses **Delete document**, then **Confirm delete**. It can't be undone. Members get "This action requires the 'admin' role in your shop."

### How do I correct an invoice amount or total?
~ correct an amount, fix an invoice total, wrong total, wrong amount, fix the balance due, wrong tax, money to check, invoice amount wrong, correct the total, fix money, wrong subtotal, balance due wrong, edit invoice, money on this document, amount paid wrong, fix the price, invoice total is wrong, the invoice total is wrong how do i fix it, total is wrong, the total is wrong
!covers:A-MONEY-FIX
Open the invoice. **Money on this document** lists Number, Date, Due, Subtotal, Tax, Total, Paid, Balance due and Status. Press **Correct <field>** beside a value, type the right one and save. The **Money to check** chip lists invoices where the numbers didn't add up.

### What are shop records and Hide shop records?
~ shop records, hide shop records, internal documents, our own paperwork, shop paperwork, internal type, internal documents in inbox, why are my own documents here, documents about my shop, team only documents, technician filter shop records
!covers:A-HIDE-SHOP
**Shop records** are documents about your own shop (typed Internal) rather than a customer's job. They have their own chip in Needs you, with an optional technician filter. Tick **Hide shop records** to keep them out of the other chips.

### What does Find reminders do?
~ find reminders, reminders, reminder, open reminders, next visit reminder, note for next visit, remind me next visit, reminder on customer, attach reminder, service reminder
!covers:A-REMINDERS
On the **Needs linking** chip, **Find reminders** scans the unlinked documents for notes like "check the filter next visit" and attaches them to the right customer. A customer's profile lists them under **Open reminders**.

### How do I answer a question about a scan on my phone?
~ needs your input, needs 1 answer, answer on phone, phone document needs input, fix on my phone, needs an answer, answer a question on the phone, all set nothing else needs an answer, mobile fix, mobile needs info, docs tab needs input, phone verify
!covers:A-M-NEEDS-INFO
Open the document from **Docs** (or tap "needs 1 answer" on a scan result). Under **Needs your input** tap the right option or type the value. You'll see "All set — nothing else needs an answer on this document." Bigger clean-up (linking, merging, deleting) is on the desktop.
