If your company is set up as Property management, Donovan answers questions about vendor insurance certificates, leases, the rent roll, work orders, vendor contracts, inspections and invoices, from your own documents, with the page.

### What property management questions can Donovan answer?
~ property questions, vendor COI expired, leases ending, who lives in unit, vacant units, open work orders, vendor contract monthly amount, failed inspections, unpaid invoices, total spent per property, needs attention card
Ask "Which vendor insurance certificates are expired?", "Which leases end in the next 60 days?", "Who lives in unit 4B?", "Which units are vacant?", "Which work orders are open?", "Which invoices are overdue?" or "What needs attention?" A vendor's current certificate is its latest one (expiring today is not expired), and an invoice is overdue only when unpaid and past the due date printed on it. Donovan never gives legal advice or judges whether coverage meets your requirements.
