DeepWell is a hosted records system for HVAC contractors. It reads a shop's own work orders, warranty sheets, invoices and nameplate photos, links them together, and lets the team ask questions in plain language. Every answer shows its source.

### What is DeepWell?
~ what does deepwell do, what do you do, tell me about deepwell, about deepwell, explain deepwell, what is this, what is this product, what is this company, overview, what does your product do, what is deepwell technology
DeepWell finds the answer in your own records. Your work orders, warranty sheets, invoices and nameplate photos go in, DeepWell reads them and links each fact to the right customer and piece of equipment, and your team asks questions the way they would ask a coworker. Every answer shows the source document. We set it up for you, so day one is already useful.

### Who is DeepWell for?

DeepWell is built for HVAC contractors today, from an owner-operator to a multi-crew shop. Office staff and dispatch use it to answer callers, technicians use it in the field on a phone, and owners use it to see expiring warranties and maintenance agreements.

### How does DeepWell work?
~ how does it work, how it works, how does deepwell work, ingest link ask, walkthrough, explain how, capture, process, steps, workflow
Three steps. **Ingest:** paper, PDFs, spreadsheets and photos go in as they are. **Link:** each document is read for serials, addresses, dates, names and amounts, and connected to the matching customer and equipment. **Ask:** you type a question and get the answer with the source page attached. Uncertain reads are flagged for a quick human check.

Work orders, warranty cards and registrations, invoices, startup sheets, permits, dispatch notes, service tickets and nameplate photos. DeepWell reads handwritten and low-quality scans too. Nothing has to be renamed or filed first.
