Team is admin only in the header; its cards are Phone app for your team, Follow-ups, Settings and Support access, plus the invite form and, below it, our sign-in provider's panel with **Members** and **Invitations** tabs (change a role, remove someone, cancel an invitation).

### Where is the QR code to put the app on my team's phones?
~ qr code, phone app for your techs, install link, copy install link, see install steps, send my tech the app, get the app on my techs phones, share the phone app, tech install link, how do my techs get the app, phone app card, how do i get my techs the app, get the app to my techs, get my techs the app, phone app for your team members, send my team member the app, get the app on my team members phones, team member install link, how do my team members get the app, how do i get my team members the app, get the app to my team members, get my team members the app
!covers:A-PHONE-CARD
An admin opens **Team → Phone app for your team**: a QR code they can scan, **Copy install link** to text or email, and **See install steps** for iPhone and Android.

### How do I revoke support access?
~ revoke support access, turn off support access, stop support access, cancel support access, end support access early, access active until, revoke now, remove support access, take away access, support still has access
!covers:A-SUPPORT-REVOKE
An admin opens **Team → Support access**. While a grant is active it says "Access active until <date>"; press **Revoke now**.

### How do I empty all of my documents but keep the company?
~ empty documents, delete all documents, empty this shops documents, delete all my files, wipe documents, clear all documents, remove every document, start over with documents, delete everything but keep account, reset documents, empty documents button, delete all my documents but keep my account, delete all documents keep my account, keep my account, empty this companies documents
!covers:A-DELETE-ALL-DOCS
An admin opens **Records → Documents**, finds "Empty this company's documents", types **DELETE** and presses **Empty documents**. It is permanent, so export first (**Team → Settings → Your data**). To remove just one, use **Delete document** on it. Members don't see **Empty documents** or **Delete document**; ask an admin.
