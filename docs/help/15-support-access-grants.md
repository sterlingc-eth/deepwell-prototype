DeepWell support does not have standing access to your documents. You decide if and when.

### How does support access work?

!covers:A-SUPPORT-GRANT
An admin opens **Team → Support access** (a collapsed card), picks **Grant for** 24 hours, 72 hours or 7 days (the maximum), adds an optional reason (up to 500 characters) and presses **Grant support access**. It is off by default and nothing creates a grant automatically.

### How does support access work?
~ support access, grant support access, give support access, let support see, temporary access, allow deepwell access, grant access to deepwell, staff access, how to give access, support login, can support see my account, help me troubleshoot, remote access, support permission
Our support team does not have standing access to your documents. When you want our help, for example troubleshooting an issue, you grant temporary support access from your account settings, and you can revoke it at any time.

### Can I see what support looked at?
~ access log, activity log, audit log, who looked at my data, who accessed my data, staff activity, see access history, access history, what did support see, log of access, accessed by, who from your team looked at my account, who looked at my account, team looked at my account, who from your team, can i see what support accessed, what support accessed, see what support accessed, what did support access, what did support look at, activity log of support, who at deepwell looked at my data, who at deepwell looked at my account
!covers:A-SUPPORT-LOG
Yes. **Team → Support access → Access log (N)** lists every staff access: action, time, record count and, for emergency access, an "emergency" marker and the reason. It never shows the records themselves. Before any access it says "No staff access recorded yet." Uploads, edits and exports are in the account activity log too.

### Can I see what support looked at?
~ access log, activity log, audit log, who looked at my data, who accessed my data, staff activity, see access history, access history, what did support see, log of access, accessed by, who from your team looked at my account, who looked at my account, team looked at my account, who from your team, can i see what support accessed, what support accessed, see what support accessed, what did support access, what did support look at, activity log of support
Support has no standing access; access happens only under a grant you give. Every grant, and everything accessed under it, is recorded in your account's activity log, so there is a record of who looked at what, and when. Uploads, edits, exports and support access are all written to that log.

### What is emergency or break-glass access?
~ break glass, emergency access, no grant, without permission, without a grant, urgent access, incident access, override, staff without grant, access without approval
For a genuine incident where you need help right now and no admin can be reached, staff can proceed without a grant only with an explicit, logged reason. It always shows up in your Access log. It's trust-based and not pre-approved, and that is one of the gaps we list openly.
