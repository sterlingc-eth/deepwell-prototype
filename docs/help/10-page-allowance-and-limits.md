Each plan has a monthly allowance of new pages, a documents-stored limit and a login limit. Donovan questions are not limited.

### How many pages can I scan per month?
~ pages per month, shop plan pages, team plan pages, page allowance, monthly pages, page limit, how many pages, how many documents, scan limit, scan allowance, scan volume, how much can i upload, upload limit, monthly upload limit, monthly scan, new pages
Solo: {{solo.pages}} new pages a month. Team: {{shop.pages}}. Crew: {{crew.pages}}. Fleet: {{fleet.pages}}. A page is one page of a document: for a PDF, one printed page; a photo is one page. A Word (.docx) file counts about one page for every 3,000 characters of text (about one printed page), at least one. An Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file counts one page for each block of roughly 6,000 characters of a sheet (the header row is repeated in every block), and every sheet that has anything in it counts at least one; an empty sheet counts none. Plain text, Markdown and JSON count one page for every 6,000 characters. Signed in, ask me and I'll check your usage.

### How are pages counted for Word, Excel and CSV files?
~ how are pages counted, how do pages count for word, how do pages count for excel, how many pages is my spreadsheet, how many pages is a word document, page count word, page count excel, page count csv, csv pages, spreadsheet pages, word pages, estimate pages, pages estimate, page estimate upload, why did my page count change, pages used by a spreadsheet, what counts as a page
A Word (.docx) file counts about one page per 3,000 characters of text, at least one. An Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file counts one page per block of roughly 6,000 characters of a sheet (header row repeated in each block); an empty sheet counts none. The real count is set when the file is read. Before that DeepWell uses a size estimate, which errs high for files full of pictures (not read or counted) and can be much lower for very repetitive files. The final count is checked at reading: a file that would use more pages than you have left this month is not added and nothing is counted. Only the final count is added to your monthly pages.

### What happens when I reach my page limit?
~ reach my limit, hit the limit, monthly page limit reached, over the page limit, out of pages, exceeded allowance, limit reached, out of scans, ran out of pages, more pages, need more pages, upgrade for more pages, page cap
!covers:A-UPLOAD-ERR
New uploads pause with a message that the monthly page limit is reached. Existing records and Donovan keep working. Upgrade your plan for a larger allowance, or email support@deepwelltechnology.com. If you have a large backlog, Records Rescue can scan it separately.

### Do questions to Donovan count against my allowance?
~ do questions count, asks count, donovan usage, does asking cost pages, question allowance, do searches count, do i pay per question
No. Donovan is unlimited on every plan. Only new pages you scan or upload count toward the monthly page allowance.

### What does "Donovan is seeing unusually high usage" mean?

It is the fair-use safety limit, which exists to stop runaway scripts. Ordinary use doesn't reach it. Email support@deepwelltechnology.com and we'll sort it out.
