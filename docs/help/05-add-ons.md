Three things are sold on top of a plan: Records Rescue scanning, the automatic outreach add-on, and API access, which comes with Fleet.

### What is Records Rescue?
~ records rescue, scanning service, scan my paper, digitize my records, paper records, boxes of paper, filing cabinet, scan my files, bulk scanning, backfile conversion, we scan for you, old paperwork, scan service, digitization, records rescue cost, records rescue price, records rescue scanning cost, scanning cost per page, cost per page, price per page, scan all my old paper files, scan my old paper, old paper files, scan paper files for me, scan my paper for me
Records Rescue is our scanning service for old paper. Full service is **${{rescue.rate}} per page with a ${{rescue.min}} minimum**. It is billed separately from your plan and can be bought under Billing. It is non-refundable once scanning has begun. You can start with a free sample box first.

### Can I try Records Rescue before paying?
~ free sample box, sample box, free demo box, try records rescue, one box, digitize free, free digitization, send a box, mail a box, send you my paper
Yes. Send one sample folder or box of old invoices, tickets or warranties and we digitize it free so you can see the result before committing to the rest. Email hello@deepwelltechnology.com to arrange it.

### What is the automatic outreach add-on?
~ outreach add-on, outreach auto, auto send, automatic emails, warranty upsell emails, extended warranty emails, automatic outreach, unattended sending, outreach automation, autopilot outreach
DeepWell can draft warranty-upsell emails to your customers when a unit's warranty is about to end. In review mode a person approves and sends each one, and that needs no add-on. The fully automatic, unattended sending is a paid add-on. For its price, email hello@deepwelltechnology.com.

### Is API access an add-on?
~ api, api access, api keys, rest api, integrate, integration, connect my systems, developer, developers, webhook, connect to quickbooks, connect to my software, integrations
API access is included on the **Fleet** plan only. Fleet admins create and revoke API keys in the app. Other plans can't create keys. Ready-made integrations with other software are not listed as available today.

### Does DeepWell integrate with QuickBooks or other software?

No built-in integrations are listed as available today. Google Drive sync and email or folder intake are marked coming soon, with no date. You can export CSV for dispatch and accounting, and Fleet has API access.
