The desktop app has four main screens in the top bar (Ask, Dashboard, Inbox, Records) plus Billing, Team and sign out. The phone app has three tabs: Ask, Scan and Docs.

### What are the main screens and where do I find things?
~ main screens, navigation, where is everything, where do i find, where is, menu, top bar, header, nav bar, screens, what are the tabs, app layout, how do i get around, find my way around, tour of the app, what does the app have, sections of the app, where is it, i cant find, cant find anything, what is on the dashboard vs records
!covers:A-NAV
Desktop top bar: **Ask** (questions to Donovan), **Dashboard** (warranty alerts and data health), **Inbox** (add files and things that need you), **Records** (customers, documents, grid, graph). At the right: the bell, **Billing**, **Team** and **Donovan** (admins only), Field/Office view, the company switcher and **Sign out**. On a narrow window only icons show. Phone: **Ask**, **Scan** and **Docs** tabs at the bottom.

### What does the number on Inbox mean?
~ number on inbox, inbox badge, inbox number, red number, badge on inbox, what is the number next to inbox, count on inbox, items needing attention, why does inbox have a number, notification number, needs your attention count
!covers:A-NAV
It is how many documents need a person right now, the same count as **Inbox → Needs you** and the **Needs a person** chip. It drops as you answer questions and resolve the flagged items.

### What is Field view and Office view, and how do I switch between light and dark?
~ field view, office view, dark mode, light mode, big text, larger text, outdoors, sunlight, bright, screen too dark, screen too small, make text bigger, theme, switch to dark, switch to light, night mode, change the theme, screen brighter, brighter screen, make the screen brighter, make the screen brighter for outside, dark theme, light theme, turn on the dark theme, how do i turn on the dark theme, switch theme
!covers:A-FIELDVIEW
**Office view** is dark; **Field view** is light with larger type so it reads outdoors. Tap the sun or moon button in the top bar (desktop) or the header (phone) to switch. The choice is remembered on that device. Field view also gives the Ask screen field-style example questions.

### Where is the DeepWell Help chat?
~ help chat, help button, support chat, where is help, where is the chatbot, chatbot, how do i get help, get help in the app, contact support in the app, talk to help, round button, bottom right button, deepwell help, help assistant, live chat, support bubble, where do i ask for help
!covers:A-HELPCHAT
Desktop: the round button at the bottom right of every screen, titled **DeepWell Help**. Phone: the same round button in the header. It answers questions about using DeepWell (not about your own records, which go to Ask). If it can't help, use **Send this to a person** and the team replies by email.

### Is there a quick way to jump to a customer, serial or screen?
~ shortcut, keyboard shortcut, quick search, jump to, command palette, ctrl k, cmd k, control k, search everything, global search, search box, universal search, find a customer fast, find a serial fast, quick find, jump to a customer, hotkey, where is search, where did search go
!covers:A-PALETTE
On the desktop press **Ctrl+K** (or **Cmd+K** on a Mac) to open "Jump to a customer, address, serial, document, or screen…". Type a few letters, use the arrow keys and **Enter**. Results are grouped: Screens, Customers, Addresses, Units, Service visits, Technicians, Documents, Ask. **Esc** closes it. The old separate Search screen was folded into this and the search boxes in Records.

### How do I share or bookmark a link to a unit or a screen?
~ share a link, copy link, link to a unit, link to a record, bookmark, deep link, send a link, share a unit, copy a link to, url for a unit, open billing link, share with my tech, send my tech a link, share with my team member, send my team member a link
!covers:A-DEEPLINK
In the Dashboard's warranty table each unit has **Copy link**; anyone signed in to your company who opens it lands on that unit. Links with `?screen=billing` open Billing. For an answer, use **Share** on the Ask screen.

### Why does my screen look different from the phone app?
~ phone vs desktop, desktop vs phone, difference between phone and desktop, what is missing on the phone, what can i do on the phone, phone app limits, cant do on phone, lite app, mobile limits, why is the phone app missing, no dashboard on phone, what can the phone app not do, what cant the phone app do, phone app not do
!covers:A-M-LIMITS
The phone app is for the field: **Ask**, **Scan** and **Docs**. Dashboard, the Inbox review queue, Team, Billing, exports and settings are desktop only. Sign out and switching companies are on the phone too, under the round profile button at the top right.

### Can DeepWell schedule jobs, take payments, text customers or change my logo?
~ schedule jobs, scheduling, dispatch, schedule a technician, take a payment, credit card payment from a customer, pay my tech, payroll, text a customer, sms, gps, track trucks, print a label, shipping label, change the logo, change my company name, branding, white label, logo on invoices, company name on the login page, recurring maintenance agreements, integrate with servicetitan, connect to quickbooks, is there a setting for, pay my techs, pay my tech through the app, pay through the app, pay my guys, pay employees, run payroll, timesheets, time clock, track hours, clock in, pay my team member, pay my team members, pay my team member through the app
DeepWell is a records system: it reads your paperwork, links it to customers and equipment, answers questions and watches warranties. It has no job scheduling or dispatch, payments, payroll, GPS tracking, customer texting, shipping labels, or branding or logo settings. The customer emails it drafts are warranty outreach (Dashboard → Open Outreach). If something you need is missing, tell support@deepwelltechnology.com.
