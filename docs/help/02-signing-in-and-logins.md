People sign in with their own DeepWell account. A shop is one account, and the owner invites everyone else from the Team screen.

### How many logins does my plan include?
~ how many users, how many seats, how many people, how many technicians, how many techs, number of logins, user limit, seat limit, login limit, how many employees, users per plan, seats per plan, login cap, how many users can i add, users can i add, how many people can i add, how many people can use it
Solo includes up to {{solo.logins}} logins, Shop up to {{shop.logins}}, Crew up to {{crew.logins}}, and Fleet {{fleet.logins}}. The owner account is not counted. So a Shop can have the owner plus five more people.

### Is the owner counted as a login?
~ owner counted, does the owner count, owner account, does the owner use a seat, main account, admin count
No. The owner account does not count toward the login limit on any plan.

### How do I invite a technician or teammate?
~ invite, add a user, add a tech, add technician, add employee, add someone, add a member, send invite, invite team, invite my tech, give access, share access, new hire, office manager login, let my office manager, office manager have a login, add my office manager, own login, separate login, login for my office manager, login for my dispatcher, add my dispatcher, add my wife, add my partner
The shop owner or an admin opens **Team** in the desktop app and invites them by email. Once they accept, they can sign in on the computer or install the phone app. Invites are checked against your plan's login limit.

### What are the roles, admin and member?
~ roles, permissions, admin role, member role, what can members do, who can see billing, who can export, who can delete, who is an admin, access levels
Two roles. **Admins** can manage billing, invite people, export or delete the shop's data and grant support access. **Members** can ask Donovan, upload and browse records. Members cannot change billing or team settings.

### Can I go over my login limit?
~ over the limit, exceed logins, more logins, need more users, too many users, seat limit reached, upgrade for more logins, add more seats, extra logins, hit my login limit, login limit reached, reached my login limit, what happens at the login limit, hit the login limit
The invite is blocked once you reach your plan's limit, and the message says so. Upgrade to a larger plan to invite more people. Fleet has no DeepWell login cap.

### How do I sign in or reset my password?
~ cannot sign in, cant log in, forgot password, reset password, password, login problem, locked out, sign in issue, log in, where do i log in, sign in page, how to sign in, how do i log in
Go to deepwelltechnology.com and choose **Log in**, or open deepwelltechnology.com/app. Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider. Use the password option on the sign-in page. If you are still locked out, email support@deepwelltechnology.com from the address on your account. Never send a password in chat or email.
!handoff:sign-in-help

### Does DeepWell charge per technician?
~ per tech, per technician, per user pricing, per seat pricing, per head, charged per person
No. Plans are priced by team size tier, not per technician. Each plan has a set number of logins, a storage limit and a monthly scan allowance.
