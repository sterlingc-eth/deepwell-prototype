DeepWell watches your records for expiring coverage and missing information, and tells the right people.

### How do warranty alerts and the daily digest work?
~ warranty alerts, notifications, bell icon, daily digest, digest email, warranty expiring, expiry alerts, expiration notifications, email digest, alerts, turn off digest, turn on digest, stop emails, unsubscribe digest, warranty reminders, bell, notification bell, mark all read, email me warranty digests, turn off the digest, what is the bell for, bell for, what is the bell, turn off the daily email, how do i turn off the daily email, daily email, stop the daily email
!covers:A-DIGEST,A-BELL
The **bell** in the header shows warranty and attention alerts with a count; click one to jump to it, or **Mark all read**. The daily digest email is a shop-wide switch: an admin turns **Email me warranty digests** on or off in **Team → Settings → Notifications**. One email a day, only when something needs attention, sent to shop admins from alert@deepwelltechnology.com.

### What is outreach?
~ outreach, warranty upsell, extended warranty email, customer emails, customer outreach, draft emails, upsell, email my customers, send emails to customers, follow up with customers, drafts, open outreach, generate drafts, approve, send approved, review first, automatic, sent log, needs an email on file, email customers about expiring warranties, email my customers about warranties, email customers about warranties
!covers:A-OUTREACH
Open **Dashboard → Open Outreach** (or **Open in Outreach** on an alert). Press **Generate drafts**, open a draft, then **Approve** (or **Approve all**); **Copy email** or **Open in mail** to send it yourself, or **Send approved (N)** when email sending is set up (admin). **Skip** drops one and **Sent log** lists what went out. In the collapsed Settings, choose **Review first** or **Automatic** (paid add-on; ask hello@deepwelltechnology.com). Only admins change settings.

### What are technician follow-ups?
~ technician follow ups, tech follow ups, missing info follow up, follow up with techs, ask the tech, missing serial, tech questions, follow ups, send follow up messages, currently due, send now, follow ups card, turn on follow up messages to techs, how do i turn on follow up messages to techs, follow up messages, follow up messages to techs
!covers:A-FOLLOWUPS
When a document is missing something only the technician knows, such as a serial, DeepWell can send a follow-up. An admin opens **Team → Follow-ups**, turns on **Send follow-up messages** (and **Also email** if wanted), and works the "Currently due" list with **Send now**, **Copy** or **Open in mail**.

### Can members see billing or change team settings?
~ can members see billing, member permissions, who can change settings, admin only, can techs see billing, restrict access, limit access, who can access billing, this action requires the admin role, requires the admin role in your shop, why does it say admin role
Members can open Billing but can't change anything. Billing, invites, data export or deletion, support access, merging customers and sending outreach are admin only; a member who clicks one gets "This action requires the 'admin' role in your shop." Members can ask Donovan, upload, browse and fix records. Ask an admin, or have one change your role in **Team**.
