DeepWell watches your records for expiring coverage and missing information, and tells the right people.

### How do warranty alerts and the daily digest work?
~ warranty alerts, notifications, bell icon, daily digest, digest email, warranty expiring, expiry alerts, expiration notifications, email digest, alerts, turn off digest, turn on digest, stop emails, unsubscribe digest, warranty reminders
DeepWell watches warranty expirations for every unit. You get a bell alert in the app and an optional daily digest email. An admin can turn the digest on or off in **Team → Settings**. Emails come from alert@deepwelltechnology.com.

### What is outreach?
~ outreach, warranty upsell, extended warranty email, customer emails, customer outreach, draft emails, upsell, email my customers, send emails to customers, follow up with customers, drafts
When a unit's warranty is about to end, DeepWell drafts the customer's extended-warranty email. An admin approves and sends each one. Fully automatic sending is a paid add-on; ask hello@deepwelltechnology.com about it.

### What are technician follow-ups?
~ technician follow ups, tech follow ups, missing info follow up, follow up with techs, ask the tech, missing serial, tech questions, follow ups
When a document is missing something only the technician knows, such as a serial number, DeepWell can send that technician a follow-up asking for it. The settings are in the app.

### Can members see billing or change team settings?
~ can members see billing, member permissions, who can change settings, admin only, can techs see billing, restrict access, limit access, who can access billing
No. Billing, invites, data export or deletion and support access are admin-only. Members can ask Donovan, upload and browse records.
