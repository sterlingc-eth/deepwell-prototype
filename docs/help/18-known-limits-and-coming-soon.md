These items are marked coming soon on the website, or are called out as not done in our security notes. I can't promise dates or that they will ship.

Nothing here has a date, and I can't promise one. Live today: ask donovan, phone app scanning, warranty tracking and export, records search, help chat. Marked **coming soon** on the site, being built next: email and folder upload, google drive sync, branch scoping, callback analytics, backups and per-company restore, public status page and uptime sla. Later, not available today: electrical, plumbing, property management, fleet and equipment rental.

### Does DeepWell make backups?
~ backups, backup, restore, off site backups, disaster recovery, recover my data, restore data, data loss, point in time restore, do you back up my data, rpo
Daily off-site backups with a 30-day restore are marked coming soon on the Crew plan, and 6-hourly backups with per-company restore on Fleet. They aren't available as a feature today. Our database provider keeps its own point-in-time history, which ages out on its own retention window. You can export your data yourself any time.

### What are the known security gaps?
~ security gaps, what is not done, limitations security, weaknesses, missing security, not yet done, what security is missing, breach notification, per tenant keys, honest about security, what dont you have
Openly: no SOC 2 or equivalent certification yet, no per-tenant encryption keys (isolation is enforced by the database), no formal published breach-notification deadline, and emergency support access is trust-based but always logged and visible to you. Bundling all original files into one zip export is not built yet either.

DeepWell is a web app. On a computer, use deepwelltechnology.com/app. The phone app installs from your browser and isn't in an app store.
