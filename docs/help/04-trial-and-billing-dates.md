The free trial is on Solo only. Billing has four states: trialing, active, past due and canceled.

### Is there a free trial?
~ free trial, trial, try for free, try it free, 30 day trial, trial period, how long is the trial, can i try deepwell, test drive, try before buying, is there a free plan, free version, free tier, is it free
Yes. Solo has a **30-day free trial**. A card is required, you won't be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel anytime. Team, Crew and Fleet do not have a trial. If you want to see DeepWell on your own records first, ask about the free sample box.

### Do I need a credit card for the trial?
~ card required, credit card for trial, need a card, card needed, payment method trial, charged during trial, when will i be charged, will i be charged, trial card, when will i be charged after the trial ends, charged after the trial, charged when the trial ends, billed after trial, when am i charged, charged right away, charged when i sign up, charged immediately, charged today, charged at signup, billed right away, billed when i sign up, will i get charged right away
Yes, a card is required at checkout for the Solo trial. You won't be charged until the 30 days are over, and you can cancel before then.

### Can I get a second free trial?
~ second trial, another trial, trial again, extend trial, extend my trial, trial extension, trial expired, trial ended, restart trial, resubscribe trial
The free trial is one per account. Canceling and signing up again does not start a new one. For an extension request, email support@deepwelltechnology.com.
!handoff:trial-extension

### What happens if my payment fails?
~ payment failed, failed payment, card declined, declined card, past due, payment problem, billing failed, card expired, update card, update payment method, change card, new credit card, invoice unpaid, what if my card gets declined, card gets declined, card declined, card fails, card doesnt work, payment declined, card was declined, my card fails, credit card declined
You keep full access for a **7-day grace period** after the due date. After that, uploads pause, but you can still read and ask Donovan about your existing records. Update your card from **Billing → Manage billing** (admins only) to restore full access.
!handoff:payment-failed

### What happens when I cancel?
~ what happens if i cancel, after canceling, cancellation effect, lose access, my data after cancel, cancel subscription effect, access after cancellation, end of billing period
Cancellation on a monthly plan takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Once a subscription has ended, you'll be asked to choose a plan to keep uploading and asking questions. You can export your data first; see the export article.

### Where can I see my renewal or trial end date?
~ renewal date, next billing date, when does my trial end, trial end date, when will i be billed, next charge, billing date, billing cycle, when does my plan renew, when do i get charged
Open **Billing** in the app. It shows your plan, status, trial end or renewal date and this month's usage. Admins can also open **Manage billing** for invoices and payment methods. If you ask me while signed in, I can look up your plan and dates.

### What does "Choose a plan to get started" mean?
~ choose a plan to get started, choose a plan, subscription required, blocked, cannot upload, cannot ask, access blocked, account locked, no plan, no subscription, need to subscribe
Your account has no active plan, either because you haven't subscribed yet or the subscription has ended. Open **Billing** and pick a plan. Until then only Billing and Team are available.
