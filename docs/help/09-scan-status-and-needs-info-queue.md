Every document moves through five stages. When DeepWell is unsure about something it asks you a short question instead of guessing.

### What do the document stages mean?
~ stages, document stage, received, read, mapped, linked, verified, what does read mean, what does mapped mean, what does linked mean, what does verified mean, status meaning, processing steps, pipeline stages, uploaded, sorted, matched, checked, stage pill, what does sorted mean, what does matched mean, what does checked mean, ai verified
!covers:A-UPLOAD-STATUS
**Uploaded:** the file arrived. **Sorted:** DeepWell worked out what kind of document it is. **Read:** the text was read and facts such as serials, dates and amounts pulled out. **Matched:** the document was connected to the right customer and equipment. **Checked:** every required fact is present and confident, or a person confirmed it (an **AI verified** badge means DeepWell's own re-check passed).

### My document is stuck or still processing. What should I do?
~ stuck, still processing, not processing, never finished, stuck at received, taking too long, upload stuck, hasnt finished, stuck on read, pending, waiting, spinning, nothing happened, document not showing
Give it a few minutes; large files and batches take longer. Check **Inbox** after a refresh. If a document has sat at **Uploaded** for more than about 15 minutes, email support@deepwelltechnology.com with the file name and the time you uploaded it. Files that were accepted but not finished show "Still processing"; the status lives in Inbox.
!handoff:stuck-document

### Why was my document flagged?
~ why flagged, why was it flagged, why does it need review, why is it flagged for review, flagged for review, low confidence, unsure, unclear, could not read, cant read, blurry, missing serial number, what does uncertain mean
DeepWell flags a document when a required fact is missing or unclear, for example a smudged nameplate or two possible customers. Open **Inbox → Needs you**, open the document, confirm or fix the value, and it moves on to **Checked**.
