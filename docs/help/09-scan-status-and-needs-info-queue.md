Every document moves through five stages. When DeepWell is unsure about something it asks you a short question instead of guessing.

### What do the document stages mean?
~ stages, document stage, received, read, mapped, linked, verified, what does read mean, what does mapped mean, what does linked mean, what does verified mean, status meaning, processing steps, pipeline stages
**Received:** the file arrived. **Read:** the text was read. **Mapped:** facts such as serials, dates and amounts were pulled out. **Linked:** the document was connected to the right customer and equipment. **Verified:** every required fact is present and confident, or a person confirmed it.

### My document is stuck or still processing. What should I do?
~ stuck, still processing, not processing, never finished, stuck at received, taking too long, upload stuck, hasnt finished, stuck on read, pending, waiting, spinning, nothing happened, document not showing
Give it a few minutes, since large files and batches take longer. If a document has been sitting at **received** for more than about 15 minutes, re-check Inbox after a refresh. If it is still stuck, email support@deepwelltechnology.com with the file name and the time you uploaded it.
!handoff:stuck-document

### What is the needs-info queue?
~ needs info, needs information, needs a person, needs review, needs attention, review queue, questions from deepwell, exception queue, intake queue, flagged documents, what does needs review mean, missing info, missing information
When DeepWell can't confidently read a fact, such as a blurry serial number or an unclear address, it leaves a short question in your queue in Inbox. Answer it, dismiss it or snooze it. Your corrections make later reads sharper.

### Why was my document flagged?
~ why flagged, why was it flagged, why does it need review, why is it flagged for review, flagged for review, low confidence, unsure, unclear, could not read, cant read, blurry, missing serial number, what does uncertain mean
DeepWell flags a document when a required fact is missing or unclear, for example a smudged nameplate or two possible customers. Open the card, confirm or fix the value, and the document moves on to verified.

### Can I correct something DeepWell read wrong?
~ correct a field, fix a mistake, wrong value, wrong serial, wrong name, wrong address, edit extracted data, edit a document, change a field, error in data, misread, incorrect data, fix wrong information, read wrong, was read wrong, serial number was read wrong, misread serial, how do i fix a wrong serial, how do i fix it
Yes. Open the document in Records or Inbox and correct the field. Your correction is used for later answers. Always check the cited source before relying on a fact for a warranty claim.
