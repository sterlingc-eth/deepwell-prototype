Your data is yours. You can take a copy at any time.

### Can I export my data?
~ export, export my data, download my data, get my data, copy of my data, data export, take my data, export everything, data portability, own my data, can i leave with my data, download everything, get a copy
Yes, anytime. A shop admin, or the owner of a solo account, can export the shop's data as one JSON file from **Team → Settings**. It includes documents, page text, extractions, customer and unit records, links and audit log entries. Exporting is admin-only and is recorded in your audit log.

### Can I get my original files back?
~ original files, download original, get my pdfs, original documents, original scans, download my files, open original, bring back my files, zip of originals, download documents
Yes, one at a time. Use **Open original** on any document to get a short-lived secure link. The data export lists every original with its file name, size and checksum. Bundling all originals into one zip is not built yet.

### Can I export to CSV for dispatch or accounting?
~ csv, csv export, export to excel, spreadsheet, export to csv, export for accounting, export for dispatch, download csv, excel, report export, export list, csv of my records, csv of my data, csv file, get a csv, records as csv, export records
Yes. DeepWell offers CSV export for dispatch and accounting, and a warranty export from the Dashboard. For anything more complex, such as a bulk API pull, Fleet has API access.

### What is the warranty export?
~ warranty export, warranty report, expiring warranties, expiring units report, warranty list, upcoming warranty expirations, warranty dashboard, expiring soon, export warranties, warranties report
The Dashboard shows warranty and maintenance expirations, and you can export the list. DeepWell also sends in-app alerts and a daily digest as coverage nears its end.
