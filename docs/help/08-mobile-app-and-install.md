DeepWell Mobile installs from your browser. There is nothing to download from an app store and nothing to update.

### Is there a DeepWell phone app?
~ phone app, mobile app, iphone app, android app, ios app, is there an app, do you have an app, app for technicians, tech app, mobile version, on my phone, mobile, use it on my phone, tablet app, ipad
Yes. DeepWell Mobile works on iPhone, iPad and Android. It has three tabs: **Ask** for Donovan, **Scan** for photographing paperwork and **Docs** for finding documents. The full desktop app has everything else: Inbox, Records, Dashboard, Team and Billing.

### How do I install the app on iPhone?
~ install on iphone, iphone install, ios install, add to home screen iphone, safari install, install ios, download for iphone, get it on iphone, ipad install, install it on my ipad, install on ipad, ipad, install on my ipad, ipad app, put it on my ipad
Open **deepwelltechnology.com/m** in **Safari**. Tap **Share**, scroll down, tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**. Open DeepWell from your home screen and sign in there. The home-screen app keeps its own sign-in.

### How do I install the app on Android?
~ install on android, android install, chrome install, add to home screen android, download for android, get it on android, install app android, install prompt, home screen on android, put it on my home screen, put this on my home screen, android home screen
Open **deepwelltechnology.com/m** in **Chrome**. Tap **Install** when DeepWell offers it, or open the menu and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**. Then open DeepWell from your home screen and sign in.

### Why isn't it in the App Store or Google Play?
~ app store, google play, play store, why not in app store, not in the app store, cant find in app store, search app store, download from store, where to download
It installs straight from the browser, so everyone always has the latest version. It gets its own icon and opens full screen like any other app. There's no store listing, so search the store won't find it. Use the install steps above.

### Does the phone app cost extra?
~ mobile app cost, phone app price, app fee, extra for app, is the app free, app included, does the app cost extra, pay for app
No. The phone app is included with every plan. Your techs sign in with their own DeepWell accounts, which the shop owner invites from Team.

### Does it work with bad signal?
~ bad signal, weak signal, offline, no signal, no service, poor connection, works offline, attic, crawlspace, slow connection, no internet, spotty coverage, rural, no cell service, cell service, crawl space, no reception, dead zone, basement, no bars, no wifi
It is built for weak signal: it opens quickly, photos are shrunk on the phone before uploading, and slow answers show a retry. Asking questions and uploading still need a connection, so it doesn't work fully offline.

### The install button doesn't show up. What do I do?
~ cant install, install button missing, no install button, install not working, add to home screen missing, cannot install, in app browser, facebook browser, open in safari, open in chrome, embedded browser, link opens in app
Open the page in **Safari** on iPhone or **Chrome** on Android. In-app browsers such as those inside social, email or messaging apps can't install DeepWell. Use the menu there and choose **Open in Safari** or **Open in Chrome**, then try again.

### How do I scan paperwork with my phone?
~ scan with phone, scan paperwork, take photo of work order, photograph document, scan tab, scan invoice, scan nameplate, snap a photo, camera scan, multiple photos, multi page, scan a work order with my phone camera, phone camera scan, scan a work order with my phone, scan with the phone camera
Open the **Scan** tab, take a photo of the work order, invoice or nameplate, and add more pages if needed. Several photos become one document, and DeepWell reads and files it. You can also choose an existing photo or PDF.
