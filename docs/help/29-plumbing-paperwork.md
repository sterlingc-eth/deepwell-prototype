If your company is set up as Plumbing, you can ask about your backflow test certificates, water heaters and their warranties, permits and inspections, sewer camera reports, service tickets, work orders, quotes, agreements, purchase orders and invoices. Answers come only from your own documents and show the page they came from.

### What plumbing questions can Donovan answer?
~ plumbing questions, backflow tests due, backflow overdue, which backflow tests failed, which permits are open, sewer camera findings
Ask things like "Which backflow tests are overdue?", "When is the next backflow test due at (an address you have on file)?", "When does the warranty on the water heater at (address) expire?", "Which permits are open?", "What did the sewer camera find at (address)?" or "What did we bill (customer name) for the water heater replacement?" Each answer lists the document and page it was read from.

### How does Donovan decide a backflow test is overdue?
~ backflow overdue, backflow due, retest, failed backflow test, latest test, older certificate
Each backflow device is tracked by its serial number and only its latest test counts; older certificates for the same device are history. A test is overdue when its next-test date is before today (due today is not overdue). A device whose latest test failed shows as needing a retest and is not listed as due.

### How does Donovan know when a water heater warranty ends?
~ plumbing water heater warranty registration, heater registration on file, no heater registration
Only when a warranty registration is on file: the expiry date printed on it is used. With no registration, Donovan does not guess a warranty from the brand or age.

### What does an open plumbing permit mean?
~ open plumbing permit meaning, plumbing permit expired, plumbing permit finished
A permit is finished when its status is final or closed (or a passed final inspection is on file), expired when it is past the printed expiry date and not finished, and open otherwise. That reflects the paperwork you uploaded, not the city's records.

### Can Donovan tell me if plumbing work meets code?
~ meets code, code compliant, will it pass, is it legal, what does the plumbing code say
No. Donovan never judges code, legal questions or whether a test or inspection should have passed. It shows what your documents say, with the page.
