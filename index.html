The problem

The knowledge exists. It's just in the wrong place.

A mid-sized shop closes 3,000 jobs a year. Each one leaves a trail — but the trail is split across a filing cabinet, a dispatcher's inbox, a shared drive, and a tech's phone. When a customer calls about a compressor installed in 2019, somebody spends forty minutes looking.

In the office

The filing cabinet

Signed work orders, manufacturer warranty registrations, permit copies. Complete, and unsearchable. The serial number you need is on page two of a carbon copy.

In the inbox

The PDF nobody renamed

Distributor invoices, startup sheets, equipment submittals — every one attached to an email titled "Re: Re: Fwd: Tuesday." Findable only by the person who received it.

In the truck

The photo on a phone

The clearest record of what was installed is a nameplate photo taken by a tech who has since moved to another company. It was never uploaded anywhere.

The platform

Ingest everything. Link what belongs together. Ask anything.

DeepWell is one hosted system that does three things to your records, continuously — from the first box of paper to this morning's job sheet. No IT department required; it runs under your company's name and we keep it running.

INGEST

Everything goes in as-is

Paper · PDF · spreadsheets · photos · email

Scan the cabinet, forward the inbox, drop the shared drive, upload from a phone in the field. Handwriting, carbon copies and nameplate photos included. Nothing has to be renamed or filed first.

  • Drag-and-drop, email intake, and folder sync
  • Reads handwritten and low-quality scans
  • Searchable within minutes of upload
LINK

Facts get connected

Equipment ↔ site ↔ customer ↔ people ↔ money

Each document is read for the facts that matter — serials, addresses, dates, names, amounts — and stitched to every other record about the same thing. One serial number pulls up seven years of history from a dozen files.

  • Every fact traceable to the page it came from
  • Uncertain reads are flagged for a quick human check
  • Your corrections make the system sharper
ASK

Answers, not search results

Desk · phone · truck

Type a question the way you'd ask a coworker. Get the answer, the linked records behind it, and the original document one tap away. Dashboards surface what's expiring, what's due, and what's slipping.

  • Plain-language questions, cited answers
  • Warranty, maintenance and callback dashboards
  • Export to CSV, your dispatch tool, or your accountant
