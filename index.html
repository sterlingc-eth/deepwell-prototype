What DeepWell does

Your paperwork, turned into answers.

Your paperwork is split across a filing cabinet, an inbox and your team's phones. DeepWell reads it all, so anyone on your team can just ask.

Made for any business with paperwork. DeepWell reads the names, addresses, dates, amounts and document numbers in invoices, contracts, forms, permits and receipts, and answers with the page attached. It works for property managers, contractors, clinics, offices and nonprofits alike. Where documents describe equipment, it goes further: warranties, serial numbers and service tickets, linked to the customer and unit they belong to.

What it's not

DeepWell is not a scheduling, dispatch, accounting or invoicing tool. Keep using the one you have; DeepWell works alongside it and is where the paperwork behind it becomes answers.

Questions owners ask

What owners ask before trusting software with their paperwork. More in our security, privacy and terms pages.

Do I have to scan everything myself?

No. Setup by our team is included with every plan: we take stock of your records, load the first batch and check the answers against the questions your office asks. For boxes of old paper, our Records Rescue service does the scanning ($0.12/page, $500 minimum), and you can send one sample box free first. After that, your team only scans new paperwork.

What if it reads something wrong?

Every answer shows the page it came from, so you can check it yourself. When DeepWell isn't sure about a read, it flags that document for a quick human check, and your corrections are used in later answers. It is AI, so it can still be wrong. That is why the source page is always attached.

Does my data train AI? Who can see it?

Your records are stored under your company's own account and are never mixed with another company's. To read pages and answer questions, DeepWell sends them to its AI providers, and under those providers' API terms that content is not used to train their models. DeepWell staff can only look at your account if your admin grants time-limited support access.

What happens if I cancel?

You can cancel any time. A monthly plan ends at the end of the current billing period (no prorated refunds for partial months), and you can cancel the free trial before you are charged. Your admin can export or delete your company's data from Team → Settings whenever they like.

Does it replace my business software?

No. DeepWell does not schedule jobs, dispatch, take payments, send invoices or keep your books, so keep the software you use now. There are no built-in integrations yet, but you can export CSV for dispatch and accounting.

Does it work on my team's phones?

Yes. The phone app installs from the browser on iPhone, iPad and Android, with no app store and no extra charge. Your team can ask questions, scan paperwork and look up documents. A scan taken with no signal is saved on the phone and uploads when it is back online; asking a question needs a connection.

Will it work for my business?

If your business keeps documents or paperwork, yes. DeepWell reads invoices, contracts, permits, forms, correspondence and photos of documents, files them by customer and address, and answers questions with the original page attached. Equipment paperwork (warranties, serials, service tickets) is where it understands the most today. Electrical, plumbing and property-management features are marked coming soon, with no dates; you can still upload your paperwork now. Not sure? Send a sample box free.

An example: coverage lost because nobody registered it

Some warranties and service agreements shrink if a registration or renewal deadline is missed. Say a 60–90 day registration window cuts a 10-year term to 5 (example terms). Then a year-6 failure — often $1,500–$4,000 (illustrative) — lands on your bill instead of the vendor's.

Your numbers will differ. DeepWell watches every expiry — bell alert plus daily digest — then drafts the customer's extended-warranty email for an admin to approve and send, or run fully automatic. Both ship today.
