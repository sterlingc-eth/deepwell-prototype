Usage: Favicon (32×32), app icon, small contexts. Geometric, scalable.

Design Rationale

  • Refrigerant Blue (#0A5F7F): Core HVAC identity—cooling systems, technical precision
  • Heating Amber (#D97706): Secondary accent for heat/maintenance contexts
  • Geometry: Coils, wells, and thermometer elements are instantly recognizable to contractors
  • No AI signaling: Purely functional design—no gradients, no gradients, no decorative flourishes
  • Scalable: Works from favicon (16px) to billboard (1000px+)
